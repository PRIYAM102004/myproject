About Uzhavan

Empowering farmers with smart technology, one field at a time.

Our Mission

Uzhavan was built with one simple goal — to bring modern technology closer to every farmer, regardless of their location or resources. We believe farming decisions should be backed by real data, not guesswork. From weather forecasts to crop recommendations, expense tracking to timely alerts, Uzhavan brings everything a farmer needs onto one simple platform.
